# On-Page SEO — 85/100

## What works

- Zero duplicate titles or descriptions across 30 pages
- All meta descriptions 132-172 chars and hand-written
- Exactly one H1 per page; OG and Twitter card tags on all pages

## Findings

### Internal link equity flows to case studies, not service pages (High)

Contextual inbound links: fiji-immigration-internal 15, prims-member-portal 10, versus angular-performance-optimization 2 and ionic-development 3

**Recommendation:** Link from each case study to the service page it demonstrates; target 6+ contextual inbound links per service page

### Five under-length titles (Low)

/resume 16 chars, /contact 17, /process 17, /insights 18, /experience 20

**Recommendation:** Extend to carry query terms within the ~60 character allowance
